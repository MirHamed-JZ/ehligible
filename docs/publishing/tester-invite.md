# Finding and inviting testers

Google needs **at least 12 testers opted in for 14 days in a row** before Eh-ligible can apply for production
([Google's rules](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465)). Aim for 15 or more,
so a few dropouts don't reset the count.

Since 2026-11-08 the testers are the members of the Google Group **Eh-ligible testers**
(`ehligible-testers@googlegroups.com`), and the closed test is open in every country. Anyone can join the group by
themselves, so there are no emails to collect: one message with three links is enough. The group is set so that
only the owner can post and see the member list.

| Step | Link |
|---|---|
| 1. Join the testers group | https://groups.google.com/g/ehligible-testers |
| 2. Become a tester (on the Android phone) | https://play.google.com/apps/testing/ca.ehligible |
| 3. Install the app | https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ca.ehligible |

Step 2 only works for accounts in the group, so testers must join with the Google account of their phone.

## 2. Post for tester-exchange communities

Developers trade tests there: they test your app for 14 days and you test theirs for 14 days. Keep your promise,
since the exchange only works if both sides stay opted in.

- Reddit: [r/AndroidClosedTesting](https://www.reddit.com/r/AndroidClosedTesting/) and
  [r/TestersCommunity](https://www.reddit.com/r/TestersCommunity/) (read each subreddit's rules first).
- The [Testers Community](https://groups.google.com/g/testers-community/about) Google Group.
- [TheClosedTest](https://theclosedtest.neerajlovecyber.com) (free; needs an account).
- Avoid paid tester services: Google can ask for more testing when testers don't really use the app, and
  emulators or duplicate accounts don't count.

**Title:** [Closed test] Eh-ligible: a countdown widget to Canadian citizenship. I'll test yours back

## 4. While the test runs

- **Count:** Play Console → Dashboard shows how many testers are opted in and the days so far.
- **Feedback log:** note each piece of feedback and what you changed (date, who, what). Google asks for a summary
  of the test when you apply for production, including how testers were recruited and how they engaged.
- **Keep testers in:** someone who leaves the group or opts out loses access, and if they come back their 14 days
  start again.
- **Updates:** new versions reach testers by themselves; the 14 days keep counting.
